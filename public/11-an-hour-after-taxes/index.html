$11 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $11 an hour brings in $22,880 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $20,216 — which is $9.72 an hour, not $11.

From $11 gross to $9.72 net

The gap is $1.28 for every hour worked, or $2,664 across the year. Effective rate: 11.6%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $678, then $236 of California income tax and $1,750 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $20,216 a year is $1,685 a month, $778 per biweekly check and $389 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $1,144 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
