$12 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $12 an hour is $24,960 a year before tax — about $2,080 a month and $480 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $21,887.

The math

$12 × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $24,960. If your schedule is different, the total moves with you:

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $480 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $12, overtime pushes the year to $29,640.

What $12 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $24,960 gross works out to about $21,887 take-home a year — $1,824 a month, or $10.52 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 12.3% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $12 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $24,960 sits below that line by $23,980, which is 51% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $12 is 1.7× the floor.
