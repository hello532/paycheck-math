$15 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $15 an hour brings in $31,200 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $26,847 — which is $12.91 an hour, not $15.

From $15 gross to $12.91 net

The gap is $2.09 for every hour worked, or $4,353 across the year. Effective rate: 14.0%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $1,564, then $402 of California income tax and $2,387 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $26,847 a year is $2,237 a month, $1,033 per biweekly check and $516 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $1,560 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
