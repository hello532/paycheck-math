$17 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $17 an hour is $35,360 a year before tax — about $2,947 a month and $680 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $30,035.

The math

$17 × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $35,360. If your schedule is different, the total moves with you:

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $680 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $17, overtime pushes the year to $41,990.

What $17 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $35,360 gross works out to about $30,035 take-home a year — $2,503 a month, or $14.44 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 15.1% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $17 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $35,360 sits below that line by $13,580, which is 72% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $17 is 2.3× the floor.
