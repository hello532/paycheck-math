$18 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $18 an hour brings in $37,440 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $31,623 — which is $15.20 an hour, not $18.

From $18 gross to $15.20 net

The gap is $2.80 for every hour worked, or $5,817 across the year. Effective rate: 15.5%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $2,313, then $640 of California income tax and $2,864 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $31,623 a year is $2,635 a month, $1,216 per biweekly check and $608 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $1,872 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
