$20,000 a Year After Taxes

On $20,000 a year, a single filer in California takes home about $17,902 — $1,492 a month, or $689 per biweekly paycheck. Total deductions come to $2,098, an effective rate of 10.5%.

What that leaves you each month

At $1,492 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $746 toward needs, $448 toward wants and $298 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $500 — the remainder after rent is $992 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $20,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $804 to your annual take-home — you keep 80.3% of it. Your top federal bracket is 10.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 19.7% rather than the bracket rate alone.
