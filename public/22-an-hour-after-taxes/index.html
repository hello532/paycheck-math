$22 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $22 an hour brings in $45,760 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $37,975 — which is $18.26 an hour, not $22.

From $22 gross to $18.26 net

The gap is $3.74 for every hour worked, or $7,785 across the year. Effective rate: 17.0%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $3,311, then $973 of California income tax and $3,501 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $37,975 a year is $3,165 a month, $1,461 per biweekly check and $730 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $2,288 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
