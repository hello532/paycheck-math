$22 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $22 an hour is $45,760 a year before tax — about $3,813 a month and $880 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $37,975.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $880 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $22, overtime pushes the year to $54,340.

What $22 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $45,760 gross works out to about $37,975 take-home a year — $3,165 a month, or $18.26 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 17.0% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $22 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $45,760 sits below that line by $3,180, which is 94% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $22 is 3.0× the floor.
