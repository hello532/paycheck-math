$23 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $23 an hour brings in $47,840 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $39,547 — which is $19.01 an hour, not $23.

From $23 gross to $19.01 net

The gap is $3.99 for every hour worked, or $8,293 across the year. Effective rate: 17.3%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $3,561, then $1,073 of California income tax and $3,660 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $39,547 a year is $3,296 a month, $1,521 per biweekly check and $761 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $2,392 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
