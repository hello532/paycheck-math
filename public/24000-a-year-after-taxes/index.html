$24,000 a Year After Taxes

On $24,000 a year, a single filer in California takes home about $21,116 — $1,760 a month, or $812 per biweekly paycheck. Total deductions come to $2,884, an effective rate of 12.0%.

What that leaves you each month

At $1,760 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $880 toward needs, $528 toward wants and $352 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $600 — the remainder after rent is $1,160 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $24,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $804 to your annual take-home — you keep 80.3% of it. Your top federal bracket is 10.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 19.7% rather than the bracket rate alone.
