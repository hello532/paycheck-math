$26 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $26 an hour is $54,080 a year before tax — about $4,507 a month and $1,040 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $44,186.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $1,040 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $26, overtime pushes the year to $64,220.

What $26 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $54,080 gross works out to about $44,186 take-home a year — $3,682 a month, or $21.24 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 18.3% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $26 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $54,080 sits above that line by $5,140, which is 111% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $26 is 3.6× the floor.
