$27 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $27 an hour brings in $56,160 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $45,732 — which is $21.99 an hour, not $27.

From $27 gross to $21.99 net

The gap is $5.01 for every hour worked, or $10,428 across the year. Effective rate: 18.6%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $4,559, then $1,572 of California income tax and $4,296 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $45,732 a year is $3,811 a month, $1,759 per biweekly check and $879 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $2,808 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
