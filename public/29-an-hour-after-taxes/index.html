$29 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $29 an hour brings in $60,320 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $48,825 — which is $23.47 an hour, not $29.

From $29 gross to $23.47 net

The gap is $5.53 for every hour worked, or $11,495 across the year. Effective rate: 19.1%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $5,058, then $1,822 of California income tax and $4,614 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $48,825 a year is $4,069 a month, $1,878 per biweekly check and $939 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $3,016 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
