$31 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $31 an hour is $64,480 a year before tax — about $5,373 a month and $1,240 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $51,890.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $1,240 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $31, overtime pushes the year to $76,570.

What $31 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $64,480 gross works out to about $51,890 take-home a year — $4,324 a month, or $24.95 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 19.5% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $31 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $64,480 sits above that line by $15,540, which is 132% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $31 is 4.3× the floor.
