$34 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $34 an hour brings in $70,720 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $55,983 — which is $26.91 an hour, not $34.

From $34 gross to $26.91 net

The gap is $7.09 for every hour worked, or $14,737 across the year. Effective rate: 20.8%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $6,728, then $2,598 of California income tax and $5,410 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $55,983 a year is $4,665 a month, $2,153 per biweekly check and $1,077 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $3,536 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
