$34,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

On a standard 40-hour week, $34,000 a year is $16.35 an hour before tax. That is $2,833 a month, $1,308 per biweekly paycheck and $654 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep about $28,997.

How the hourly rate changes with your schedule

34,000 ÷ 2,080 hours is the standard conversion for a salaried job. Salaried roles usually pay the same regardless of hours, so the real hourly rate depends on how long you actually work:

This is why two offers with the same salary can differ sharply: a role that expects 50 hours a week pays $13.08 an hour, while the same money over 40 hours is worth $16.35. Add unpaid on-call or a long commute and the gap widens again.

Is $34,000 a good salary?

The median full-time annual wage in the US is about $48,940. At $34,000 you earn 69% of the median, which is $14,940 less than the typical full-time worker. Housing usually decides whether that feels comfortable: a common budgeting rule caps rent at 30% of gross, which here is $850 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $34,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $764 to your annual take-home — you keep 76.3% of it. Your top federal bracket is 12.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 23.7% rather than the bracket rate alone.
