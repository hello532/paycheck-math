$38,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

On a standard 40-hour week, $38,000 a year is $18.27 an hour before tax. That is $3,167 a month, $1,462 per biweekly paycheck and $731 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep about $32,051.

How the hourly rate changes with your schedule

38,000 ÷ 2,080 hours is the standard conversion for a salaried job. Salaried roles usually pay the same regardless of hours, so the real hourly rate depends on how long you actually work:

This is why two offers with the same salary can differ sharply: a role that expects 50 hours a week pays $14.62 an hour, while the same money over 40 hours is worth $18.27. Add unpaid on-call or a long commute and the gap widens again.

Is $38,000 a good salary?

The median full-time annual wage in the US is about $48,940. At $38,000 you earn 78% of the median, which is $10,940 less than the typical full-time worker. Housing usually decides whether that feels comfortable: a common budgeting rule caps rent at 30% of gross, which here is $950 a month.
