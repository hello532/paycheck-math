$42 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $42 an hour brings in $87,360 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $66,240 — which is $31.85 an hour, not $42.

From $42 gross to $31.85 net

The gap is $10.15 for every hour worked, or $21,120 across the year. Effective rate: 24.2%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $10,389, then $4,048 of California income tax and $6,683 of Social Security and Medicare.

Compare by state

9 states have no wage income tax — Alaska, Florida, New Hampshire, Nevada, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, Wyoming. All of them produce the identical take-home figure at this rate, so any of those state pages shows the same number. The difference appears only in states that tax wages.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $66,240 a year is $5,520 a month, $2,548 per biweekly check and $1,274 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $4,368 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
