$45 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $45 an hour brings in $93,600 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $70,049 — which is $33.68 an hour, not $45.

From $45 gross to $33.68 net

The gap is $11.32 for every hour worked, or $23,551 across the year. Effective rate: 25.2%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $11,762, then $4,628 of California income tax and $7,160 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $70,049 a year is $5,837 a month, $2,694 per biweekly check and $1,347 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $4,680 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
