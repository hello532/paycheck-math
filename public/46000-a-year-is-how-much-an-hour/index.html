$46,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

On a standard 40-hour week, $46,000 a year is $22.12 an hour before tax. That is $3,833 a month, $1,769 per biweekly paycheck and $885 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep about $38,159.

How the hourly rate changes with your schedule

46,000 ÷ 2,080 hours is the standard conversion for a salaried job. Salaried roles usually pay the same regardless of hours, so the real hourly rate depends on how long you actually work:

Is $46,000 a good salary?

The median full-time annual wage in the US is about $48,940. At $46,000 you earn 94% of the median, which is $2,940 less than the typical full-time worker. Housing usually decides whether that feels comfortable: a common budgeting rule caps rent at 30% of gross, which here is $1,150 a month.
