$47 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $47 an hour is $97,760 a year before tax — about $8,147 a month and $1,880 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $72,589.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $1,880 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $47, overtime pushes the year to $116,090.

What $47 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $97,760 gross works out to about $72,589 take-home a year — $6,049 a month, or $34.90 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 25.7% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $47 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $97,760 sits above that line by $48,820, which is 200% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $47 is 6.5× the floor.
