What that leaves you each month

At $3,244 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $1,622 toward needs, $973 toward wants and $649 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $1,175 — the remainder after rent is $2,069 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $47,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $744 to your annual take-home — you keep 74.4% of it. Your top federal bracket is 12.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 25.6% rather than the bracket rate alone.
