$48,000 a Year After Taxes

On $48,000 a year, a single filer in California takes home about $39,666 — $3,305 a month, or $1,526 per biweekly paycheck. Total deductions come to $8,334, an effective rate of 17.4%.

What that leaves you each month

At $3,305 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $1,653 toward needs, $992 toward wants and $661 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $1,200 — the remainder after rent is $2,105 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $48,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $744 to your annual take-home — you keep 74.4% of it. Your top federal bracket is 12.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 25.6% rather than the bracket rate alone.
