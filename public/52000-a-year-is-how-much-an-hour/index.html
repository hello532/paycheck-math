$52,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

On a standard 40-hour week, $52,000 a year is $25.00 an hour before tax. That is $4,333 a month, $2,000 per biweekly paycheck and $1,000 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep about $42,640.

How the hourly rate changes with your schedule

52,000 ÷ 2,080 hours is the standard conversion for a salaried job. Salaried roles usually pay the same regardless of hours, so the real hourly rate depends on how long you actually work:

Is $52,000 a good salary?

The median full-time annual wage in the US is about $48,940. At $52,000 you earn 106% of the median, which is $3,060 more than the typical full-time worker. Housing usually decides whether that feels comfortable: a common budgeting rule caps rent at 30% of gross, which here is $1,300 a month.
