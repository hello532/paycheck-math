$59 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $59 an hour brings in $122,720 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $87,809 — which is $42.22 an hour, not $59.

From $59 gross to $42.22 net

The gap is $16.78 for every hour worked, or $34,911 across the year. Effective rate: 28.4%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $18,187, then $7,336 of California income tax and $9,388 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $87,809 a year is $7,317 a month, $3,377 per biweekly check and $1,689 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $6,136 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
