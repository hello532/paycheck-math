$60 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $60 an hour brings in $124,800 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $89,037 — which is $42.81 an hour, not $60.

From $60 gross to $42.81 net

The gap is $17.19 for every hour worked, or $35,763 across the year. Effective rate: 28.7%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $18,686, then $7,530 of California income tax and $9,547 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $89,037 a year is $7,420 a month, $3,424 per biweekly check and $1,712 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $6,240 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
