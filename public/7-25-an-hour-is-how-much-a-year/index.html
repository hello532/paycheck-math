$7.25 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $7.25 an hour is $15,080 a year before tax — about $1,257 a month and $290 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $13,831.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $290 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $7.25, overtime pushes the year to $17,908.

What $7.25 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $15,080 gross works out to about $13,831 take-home a year — $1,153 a month, or $6.65 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 8.3% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $7.25 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $15,080 sits below that line by $33,860, which is 31% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $7.25 is 1.0× the floor.
