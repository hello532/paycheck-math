The tax structure you hit at $72/hour in Illinois

Your first $16,100 of gross pay is covered by the federal standard deduction, so federal income tax starts above that line. Illinois has no standard deduction for this calculation, so state tax applies from the first dollar.

What five hours of overtime actually adds

At time-and-a-half, five extra hours a week is $28,080 more gross a year. After federal tax, Illinois tax and FICA, that lands as $17,803 — about $68.47 for each of those extra hours.

What this page does not model

Single filer, standard deduction, no 401(k), no health premium, no dependents, no local or city income tax, and state credits and personal exemptions are excluded — which means the state figure is a slight overstatement. Self-employment would double the FICA share.

What is different about Illinois

  • Social Security stops at $184,500 of wages. At $149,760 you are below that line, so the full 6.2% applies to every paycheck.
