On $77,000 a year, a single filer in California takes home about $59,899 — $4,992 a month, or $2,304 per biweekly paycheck. Total deductions come to $17,101, an effective rate of 22.2%.

What that leaves you each month

At $4,992 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $2,496 toward needs, $1,497 toward wants and $998 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $1,925 — the remainder after rent is $3,067 a month.
