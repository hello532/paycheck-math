$78 an Hour After Taxes

Working 40 hours a week at $78 an hour brings in $162,240 a year before tax. After federal income tax, state tax and FICA, a single filer in California keeps about $111,145 — which is $53.44 an hour, not $78.

From $78 gross to $53.44 net

The gap is $24.56 for every hour worked, or $51,095 across the year. Effective rate: 31.5%. Federal income tax is the largest piece at $27,672, then $11,012 of California income tax and $12,411 of Social Security and Medicare.

Monthly and weekly figures

Net pay of $111,145 a year is $9,262 a month, $4,275 per biweekly check and $2,137 a week. A 401(k) contribution of even 5% would remove $8,112 from the taxable figure before any of this is computed, which lowers the effective rate slightly.
