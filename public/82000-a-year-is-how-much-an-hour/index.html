$82,000 a Year Is How Much an Hour?

On a standard 40-hour week, $82,000 a year is $39.42 an hour before tax. That is $6,833 a month, $3,154 per biweekly paycheck and $1,577 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep about $62,968.

How the hourly rate changes with your schedule

82,000 ÷ 2,080 hours is the standard conversion for a salaried job. Salaried roles usually pay the same regardless of hours, so the real hourly rate depends on how long you actually work:

Is $82,000 a good salary?

The median full-time annual wage in the US is about $48,940. At $82,000 you earn 168% of the median, which is $33,060 more than the typical full-time worker. Housing usually decides whether that feels comfortable: a common budgeting rule caps rent at 30% of gross, which here is $2,050 a month.
