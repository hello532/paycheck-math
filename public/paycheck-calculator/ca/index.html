How California taxes wages

California uses progressive brackets, so only the income inside each band is taxed at that band's rate. On a $75,000 salary the state tax comes to $2,941, an effective 3.9% — lower than the top bracket you reach.

Federal tax applies everywhere

Whatever the state does, the federal side is identical. On $75,000 a single filer pays $7,670 of federal income tax after the $16,100 standard deduction, plus $5,738 in Social Security and Medicare.

What is specific to California here

Your marginal rate — what you lose on the next dollar earned — is 37.6% in California, against 29.6% in Texas. That is the number that matters for overtime, a raise or a second job.
