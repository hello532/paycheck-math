Where Georgia income sits in the tax structure

Your first $16,100 of gross pay is covered by the federal standard deduction, so federal income tax starts above that line. Georgia adds its own $15,000 deduction before state tax starts.

What this page does not model

Single filer, standard deduction, no 401(k), no health premium, no dependents, no local or city income tax, and state credits and personal exemptions are excluded — which means the state figure is a slight overstatement. Self-employment would double the FICA share.

What is different about Georgia

  • Social Security stops at $184,500 of wages. At $110,000 you are below that line, so the full 6.2% applies to every paycheck.
