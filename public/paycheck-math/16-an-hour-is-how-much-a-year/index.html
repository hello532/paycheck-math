$16 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $16 an hour is $33,280 a year before tax — about $2,773 a month and $640 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $28,447.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $640 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $16, overtime pushes the year to $39,520.

What $16 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $33,280 gross works out to about $28,447 take-home a year — $2,371 a month, or $13.68 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 14.5% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $16 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $33,280 sits below that line by $15,660, which is 68% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $16 is 2.2× the floor.
