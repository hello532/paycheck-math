What that leaves you each month

At $15,724 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $7,862 toward needs, $4,717 toward wants and $3,145 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $7,375 — the remainder after rent is $8,349 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $295,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $534 to your annual take-home — you keep 53.3% of it. Your top federal bracket is 35.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 46.7% rather than the bracket rate alone.
