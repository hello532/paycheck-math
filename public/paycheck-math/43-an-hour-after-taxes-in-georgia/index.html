The tax structure you hit at $43/hour in Georgia

Your first $16,100 of gross pay is covered by the federal standard deduction, so federal income tax starts above that line. Georgia adds its own $12,000 deduction before state tax starts.

What five hours of overtime actually adds

At time-and-a-half, five extra hours a week is $16,770 more gross a year. After federal tax, Georgia tax and FICA, that lands as $10,927 — about $42.03 for each of those extra hours.

What this page does not model

Single filer, standard deduction, no 401(k), no health premium, no dependents, no local or city income tax, and state credits and personal exemptions are excluded — which means the state figure is a slight overstatement. Self-employment would double the FICA share.

What is different about Georgia

  • Social Security stops at $184,500 of wages. At $89,440 you are below that line, so the full 6.2% applies to every paycheck.
