$43,000 a Year After Taxes

On $43,000 a year, a single filer in California takes home about $35,868 — $2,989 a month, or $1,380 per biweekly paycheck. Total deductions come to $7,132, an effective rate of 16.6%.

What that leaves you each month

At $2,989 net a month, the 50/30/20 rule would put $1,495 toward needs, $897 toward wants and $598 toward savings or debt. If rent takes 30% of gross — $1,075 — the remainder after rent is $1,914 a month.

What the next $1,000 does

At $43,000, another $1,000 of gross pay adds $764 to your annual take-home — you keep 76.3% of it. Your top federal bracket is 12.0%; Social Security and Medicare take another 7.6%, and California income tax a little more, which is why the marginal rate you face is 23.7% rather than the bracket rate alone.
