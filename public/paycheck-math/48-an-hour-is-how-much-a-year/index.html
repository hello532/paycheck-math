$48 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $48 an hour is $99,840 a year before tax — about $8,320 a month and $1,920 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $73,859.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $1,920 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $48, overtime pushes the year to $118,560.

What $48 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $99,840 gross works out to about $73,859 take-home a year — $6,155 a month, or $35.51 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 26.0% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $48 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $99,840 sits above that line by $50,900, which is 204% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $48 is 6.6× the floor.
