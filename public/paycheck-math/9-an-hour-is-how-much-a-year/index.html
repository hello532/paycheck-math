$9 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

At 40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year, $9 an hour is $18,720 a year before tax — about $1,560 a month and $360 a week. After estimated federal tax and FICA you keep roughly $16,873.

Most hourly jobs pay for actual hours worked, so a week off without pay removes roughly $360 from your year. Federal law (FLSA) requires 1.5× pay for hours over 40 in a workweek for most non-exempt employees; if you regularly work 45 hours at $9, overtime pushes the year to $22,230.

What $9 an hour looks like after tax

For a single filer with no dependents in California, $18,720 gross works out to about $16,873 take-home a year — $1,406 a month, or $8.11 for every hour worked. That is an effective rate of 9.9% across federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Is $9 an hour a good wage?

The median full-time annual wage in the United States is about $48,940. $18,720 sits below that line by $30,220, which is 38% of the median. On a 40-hour week the federal minimum wage of $7.25 brings in $15,080, so $9 is 1.2× the floor.
