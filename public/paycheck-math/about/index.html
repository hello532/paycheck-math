About Paycheck Math

Paycheck Math is a free calculator and reference site for US pay questions: what a salary is per hour, what a wage is per year, and what actually arrives after tax.

What we do

Figures are computed from published rates rather than copied from another calculator: federal brackets and standard deductions from the IRS, FICA and the wage base from the Social Security Administration, and state rules from the Tax Foundation's state table plus, where they changed for 2026, the state revenue department. Where a state's 2026 bracket widths were not yet published, the page says so. Sources are listed on the methodology page.

How to read the numbers

Take-home figures are estimates for a single filer with the standard deduction and no other pre-tax deductions. Real paychecks differ with 401(k) contributions, health insurance, dependents, credits and local taxes. Use these numbers to compare offers and states, then check your actual pay stub for the precise figure.

Corrections

If a rate is wrong, tell us and we will fix it. Email the contact page with the page URL and the source you are citing.
