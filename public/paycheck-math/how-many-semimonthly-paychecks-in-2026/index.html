Why the count differs by year

2026 starts on a Thursday. Whether a semimonthly schedule produces 24 checks or one more depends on where the year boundary lands relative to the payday. 52 weeks divide into 365 days with one or two days left over, which is why some years carry 27 checks instead of 26. The table above is computed from the actual calendar, not from a rule of thumb.
