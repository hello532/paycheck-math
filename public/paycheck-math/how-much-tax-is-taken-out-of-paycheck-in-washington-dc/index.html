How Much Tax Is Taken Out of a Paycheck in Washington DC?

On a typical Washington DC salary of $108,200, a single filer has about 27.2% withheld: $29,480 a year, or $2,457 a month. That ranks Washington DC 50 of 50 for paycheck withholding among the states here.

Where Washington DC sits

Washington DC ranks 50 of 50 on paycheck withholding. The states immediately around it: Hawaii at 26.5%, Oregon at 26.7%.

The lightest state measured here is Tennessee at 16.6%; the heaviest is Washington DC at 27.2%. Washington DC sits 10.7% above the lightest and 0.0% below the heaviest.

The salary used here, $108,200, is Washington DC's median household income from 2023 — so this is what a typical local earner sees, not what a national average salary would produce in Washington DC.

What actually changes your number

Three things move withholding more than the state does: filing status, pre-tax deductions and additional income. A 401(k) contribution comes out before federal income tax, so putting in 5% lowers the federal line without lowering the FICA line. Filing jointly widens the federal brackets, which is why two similar salaries can withhold very differently.
