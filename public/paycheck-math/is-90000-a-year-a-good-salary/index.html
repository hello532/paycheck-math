Is $90,000 a Year a Good Salary?

$90,000 is 184% of the US median full-time wage ($48,940), so it is strong. What that buys depends almost entirely on where you live and how much housing costs there.

How it compares

The $48,940 median covers all full-time wage and salary workers in the United States. At $90,000 you are ahead of 46% of that distribution. Two things move the answer more than the headline number: state tax and housing.

What it feels like month to month

After tax in California you keep $67,852, or $5,654 a month. The common 30% housing rule caps rent at $2,250; in a high-cost metro that is tight, in a mid-size city it is comfortable. The 50/30/20 split of net pay gives $2,827 to needs, $1,696 to wants and $1,131 to savings.

The honest answer

Whether $90,000 is "good" depends on your household size, debt and city. A single renter in a mid-cost city with no debt is comfortable; a family of four in San Francisco or New York on the same number is not.
