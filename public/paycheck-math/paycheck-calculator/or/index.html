How Oregon taxes wages

Oregon uses progressive brackets, so only the income inside each band is taxed at that band's rate. On a $80,200 salary the state tax comes to $6,444, an effective 8.0% — lower than the top bracket you reach.

Oregon taxes its first dollar at 4.8%; the band containing $80,200 starts at $0 and charges 8.8%. Going from $80,200 to $100,250 adds $1,754 of state tax.

Federal tax applies everywhere

Whatever the state does, the federal side is identical. On $80,200 a single filer pays $8,814 of federal income tax after the $16,100 standard deduction, plus $6,135 in Social Security and Medicare.

What is specific to Oregon here

Your marginal rate — what you lose on the next dollar earned — is 38.4% in Oregon, against 29.6% in Texas. That is the number that matters for overtime, a raise or a second job.

What is different about Oregon

  • Local income taxes add about 0.18% of AGI on average in Oregon, on top of the state rate. County and city taxes are excluded from the calculator above.
  • Some cities and counties levy their own payroll or wage taxes in Oregon. Those are local, not state, and excluded here.
  • Federal income tax is deductible on the state return. Oregon lets filers subtract some or all federal tax paid from state taxable income, which lowers the real state bill below what the flat table shows.
  • 4 income bands. The top band starts at $125,000 of taxable income and charges 9.9%.
  • Social Security stops at $184,500 of wages. At $80,200 you are below that line, so the full 6.2% applies to every paycheck.

Where $80,200 lands in Oregon

Oregon households have a median income of $80,160, ranking 20 of 51 jurisdictions against a national median of $77,719. A gross salary of $80,200 is 100% of the Oregon median and 103% of the national one — $40 above it.

By percentile, that puts a single earner in the 40th-60th percentile of Oregon households. Read this as a rough marker, not an exact position: the percentiles count whole households, which often have two earners, while this salary is one person's gross.

Where Oregon income sits in the tax structure

Your first $16,100 of gross pay is covered by the federal standard deduction, so federal income tax starts above that line. Oregon adds its own $2,910 deduction before state tax starts.

What this page does not model

Single filer, standard deduction, no 401(k), no health premium, no dependents, no local or city income tax, and state credits and personal exemptions are excluded — which means the state figure is a slight overstatement. Self-employment would double the FICA share.
