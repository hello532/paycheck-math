Privacy policy

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The salary you type is never sent to a server.

What we collect

Nothing you type into a calculator leaves your device — the arithmetic happens in JavaScript on the page. We do not require an account and we do not ask for an email address.

Analytics and advertising

We use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics to count page views. If advertising is enabled on a page, the ad provider may set cookies to measure and personalise ads, subject to your consent where required. You can block these with any content blocker and the calculators still work.
